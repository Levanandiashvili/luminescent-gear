Services

Mastering

Stereo masters for streaming and download, delivered as 24‑bit WAV, with 16‑bit/44.1 kHz versions on request.

Stem mastering

Mastering from grouped stems rather than one stereo file, for finer control over each part of the mix. The balance stays as you set it.

Mixing

Mixing from your multitrack session, with the master included.

Vinyl master

A dedicated master for the cut, with a centred low end, controlled sibilance and gentler limiting, sent straight to your cutting engineer or pressing plant. We don't cut lacquers.

Send your music

How it works

Email the artist name, release title, number of tracks, release date and format, with one download link to your mixes, one or two reference tracks and any notes.

Put all files for the release into one folder and zip it. Masters come back as a download, and vinyl masters go straight to your cutting engineer or pressing plant.

Preparing your mixes

Stereo WAV or AIFF at 24‑bit or 32‑bit float, at the sample rate of your session. No sample rate conversion, normalising or dither.

Peaks between −10 and −4 dBFS and never above −3 dBFS, set with channel and group faders. No limiters or clippers on the mix bus. A bus compressor that is part of the sound can stay; mention it in your notes.

A second or two of silence at each end, fades printed, files named with track number, artist and title.

Stem mastering

Start from a finished mix and split it into six to eight groups, such as kick, bass, drums, percussion, music, effects and vocals, with every effect printed.

All stems start at the same point and run the same length. Played together, they should sound exactly like your stereo mix, so send that too, one folder per song.

Vinyl

De-ess vocals carefully. Sharp esses distort on vinyl and are hard to remove from a finished mix. If you're unsure, send the vocal as a separate stem.

Add the side split and running order with the time of each side, and book your cutting engineer or pressing plant in advance.
